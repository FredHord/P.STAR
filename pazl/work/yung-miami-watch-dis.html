About the project

Creative direction for Yung Miami's Watch Dis!: the single cover, a campaign shot across Miami in gold on a white Mercedes 500 SL, and the Take Me to Chanel film.

Art direction plays a crucial role in telling an artist's story. Visuals that align with the sound connect with the audience on a deeper level.
