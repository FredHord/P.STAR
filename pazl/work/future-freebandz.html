About the project

As creative director for Future, PAZL® designed and developed merchandise across High Off Life, Pluto × Baby Pluto, the Nobody Safe Tour, the Legendary Nights Tour × Reebok and more, alongside campaign photography and film.

By applying fashion expertise to merch, the studio treats each drop as a brand in its own right, with a focus on quality and execution.
