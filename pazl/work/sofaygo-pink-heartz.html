About the project

Campaign wardrobe for SoFaygo's Pink Heartz: a black leather racing jacket with the album's thorned heart across the back and the SoFaygo wordmark on the chest, with matching leather pants piped in pink. He wore the look to perform Fasho on Jimmy Kimmel Live.

Wardrobe built for an album rollout: one look that carries the record's identity through the campaign, the press and the stage.
