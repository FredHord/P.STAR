About the project

PAZL leads the design department of the independent luxury brand Cease & Desist as its Creative Director, overseeing a portfolio that includes leather goods, denim and accessories.

Co-founded by Fred Saint Foster, the brand combines luxury fashion with streetwear, from the first sketch to the finished piece.
