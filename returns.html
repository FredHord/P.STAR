Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your P.STAR pieces. If something isn’t right, eligible items may be returned within 14 days of delivery, unworn and with original tags attached.

How to start a return

Email studio@pstar.com with your order number and reason for return. Please allow 24–48 hours for us to respond with instructions.

Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Processing times vary by provider.

Non-returnable items

Final-sale items and worn or washed garments are not eligible for return.

Exchanges

To exchange for a different size or piece, contact us and we’ll help arrange it subject to availability.
