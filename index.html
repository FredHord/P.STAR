Statement

P.STAR exists at the edge of the frame — where the body outruns the shutter and the athlete becomes a trace of force.

No color. No noise. A single grayscale register held across every garment, every image, every surface — so that motion, not decoration, carries the work. Built for those who move first and explain later.
